How to read

MR20: % of liquid stocks trading above their 20 EMA. Below 20 = heavy selling (bounce zone). Above 80 = stretched.

U4 / D4: number of stocks up / down 4% or more in a day, with volume higher than the previous day.

W20: number of stocks up 20% or more over the last 5 days. High (20+) means many stocks already ran.

Liquid stocks: average 10-day turnover above Rs 10 crore and price above Rs 10. Thresholds can be changed in CFG at the top of the script.

Educational framework, not a trading signal. Test and manage risk.
